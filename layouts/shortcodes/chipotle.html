/chipotle

Probably one of the quicker orders on the line.

Some people need a full minute at the counter. Not me. It's a short list, and I like it that way.

When I lived in DC, Chipotle was my once-a-week thing. Then I moved back home to Kentucky, and there isn't one anywhere near me. So now, whenever I spot one on the road, I stop, order my burrito, and enjoy every bite while it lasts.

How it gets built

  1. Cilantro‑lime rice

    The cozy base everything sits on.

  2. Black beans

    Always black.

  3. 2×

    Chicken

    The main event, so it gets two scoops.

  4. 2×

    Cheese

    Double. Don't be shy with it.
