How this site gets made.

Books used to end with a colophon, a little note about the typeface, the paper, and who printed it. This is that page for Toby Geeks Out! Everything behind the scenes, all in one spot.

Where it lives

The foundation everything sits on.

  • Micro.blog

    Home base. Hosting, posting, the timeline, and the newsletter.

  • Hugo

    The engine Micro.blog uses to turn my posts into pages.

  • Theme: Classic

    My own custom theme, the one you're looking at. I tinker with it almost every day, and the Tinkering page keeps track. Hutch is coming soon

  • GitHub

    Where the theme's code lives, with every change saved.

The look

Two Looks to pick from, a warm palette, and a Sky that changes through the day.

Reading text has a day weight and a night weight. When the Sky goes dark at Dusk, 70s text gets a touch heavier and Modern text gets a touch lighter, so both read comfortably on a dark page. Tap the Sky button until it says Night to see it.

Sky

The site's colors follow the sun where you are. I call it the Sky, and it moves through five stages every day. Want it to stay put? The Sky button lets you pin it to Day or Night.

  1. Dawnearly morning
  2. Daybright and clear
  3. Sunsetgolden hour
  4. Duskfirst and last light
  5. Nightlights out

The tile with the orange outline is the Sky you're seeing right now.
