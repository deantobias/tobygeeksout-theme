New to feeds?

A feed lets you follow a bunch of sites in one app, with no algorithm and no ads deciding what you see. About Feeds explains it better than I can, and it only takes a couple of minutes.

RSS works in pretty much every reader. JSON is a newer format some readers like better. Pick either one, they have the same posts.
