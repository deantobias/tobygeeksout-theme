  • Samsung M8 27-inch monitorI plug the Air into it when I want the big screen.
  • Mac mini M2Stays at the desk.

Phone

  • Google Pixel 11 Pro XLMy phone now. Before this I had the Galaxy Z Fold 7.

Browser

  • SafariSometimes, on the Mac.

Handy to have

  • Live TranscribeReal-time captions for when I need to talk with people out and about, like at the store or the pharmacy.
  • SL2T (sign to text) FavoriteGoogle’s sign-to-text on my phone. I sign my messages instead of typing them out.

Writing and reading

Photos

AI helpers

  • ChatGPTNow and then to check something, and it’s great at making AI images.

I built these

  • PerchMy own Micro.blog app. Posting, my timeline, and reading articles and blogs.

  • Focus Mail NewMy own email app, built just for me. It keeps my inbox calm and helps me find blog ideas.

Off the screen

A few LEGO sets still waiting to be built, a pile of colored pencils, and books I want to get back to. The plan is the new sunroom: no phones, no screens, just a comfy spot for reading, coloring and maybe some LEGO.
